## Lead Qualification Checklist
- Urgency trigger identified (compliance warning, board mandate, principal vacancy).
- Decision-maker verified (Superintendent, Chief Academic Officer, Charter Network Lead).
- Budget source noted (ESSER carryover, foundation grant, general fund).
- Modality fit (remote acceptable? travel reimbursement approved?).
- Pain alignment with equity turnaround, teen-parent support, or AI reporting needs.

## LinkedIn Content Cadence (for sequencing support)
- Week 1: Post about closing achievement gaps with AI dashboards.
- Week 2: Share testimonial or story about teen parents supported.
- Week 3: Publish insight from CSU East Bay thesis tying into current policy news.
- Week 4: Highlight pricing/availability reminder with Calendly link.

## Automation Hooks
- Trigger Touch 1 email when new lead status = “Qualified” in Airtable.
- Auto-populate personalization tokens from Airtable fields (pain_point, trigger_event, proof_asset).
- Log opens/replies in Airtable via Zapier; if no response after Touch 5, move to Nurture view.
